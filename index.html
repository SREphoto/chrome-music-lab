What is Chrome Music Lab?

Chrome Music Lab is a website that makes learning music more accessible through fun, hands-on web experiments.

Educators use these experiments in classrooms across the globe to explore music and its connections to science, math, physics, art, and technology.

All experiments are built with open web standards including Web Audio API, WebRTC, Tone.js, and WebGL. No signups or installation required.

Originally created by Google Creative Lab and collaborators under Apache 2.0 open source license.
