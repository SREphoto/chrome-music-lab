An Octave is the acoustic interval between a frequency and its exact double (2f). Because wave peaks align precisely every second cycle, our ears hear it as the "same" note in a higher register.

4/4 Common Time

The foundation of modern pop, rock, funk, and electronic music. Four quarter-note beats per measure with strong accents on beat 1 and backbeat on 2 and 4.

• Top Number: 4 Beats Per Bar Count 1 – 2 – 3 – 4 before the measure resets.
• Bottom Number: 4 = Quarter Note Each beat equals one quarter note (♩). 8 = Eighth (♪), 2 = Half (𝅗𝅥).
