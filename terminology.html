Pitch & Frequency

Hertz (Hz)

Pitch is how high or low a note sounds. Acoustically, it corresponds directly to Frequency — the speed of wave cycles vibrating per second in Hertz (Hz).

Octave

2:1 Mathematical Ratio

An Octave is the acoustic interval between a frequency and its exact double (2f). Because wave peaks align precisely every second cycle, our ears hear it as the "same" note in a higher register.
